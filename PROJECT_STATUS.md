## Implemented and testable

### Deterministic decision core

- Effective-date policy selection with section-level citations
- Request-date-bounded purchase history and `Decimal` median/variance arithmetic
- Finance-threshold, alternative-quote and ISO certificate rules
- Three decision outcomes: `APPROVED`, `CONDITIONAL_REVIEW`, `REJECTED`
- An inactive supplier ends in `REJECTED` with no approval opened (`SUP-COMP-2026 §2.1`)

### Human authorization and enterprise action

- Server-side role matrix for analysis, approval, execution, audit, intake and policy questions
- Typed action preview generated by the server before any write
- Atomic approval, rejection, expiry and supersede transitions under concurrency
- Approval reuse on an identical re-analysis, supersede after a corrected one
- Atomic, payload-bound idempotent MockDesk ticket creation with bounded retry
- Structured domain errors, trace IDs and audit events on success and failure paths

### Measurement

- `GET /api/v1/metrics/summary` derives pilot metrics from the audit trail itself
- Decision mix, approval outcomes, tickets, prevented duplicates, quarantines,
  denied/blocked actions and median cycle time
- Browser metrics card over the same endpoint

### Optional model layer

The layer is off unless `VALUEBRIDGE_LLM_API_KEY` is set; the whole suite runs keyless.

- Narrator: explains an already locked decision in Turkish; decision fields are
  byte-identical with the model on or off and the narrative never enters the
  approval fingerprint
- Intake assistant: `POST /api/v1/requests/intake` turns free text into a draft
  request a human must review; a detected injection pattern is flagged as data
  and never blocks or triggers the analysis
- Policy Q&A: `POST /api/v1/policies/ask` filters by effective date, role and
  trust *before* scoring, so superseded policy and untrusted supplier
  attachments never enter the candidate pool; BM25 always runs, the embedding
  layer is optional and degrades to lexical retrieval

### Independence

- MockDesk shares no module with the `app` package; an AST test enforces the
  boundary (`tests/test_mockdesk_independence.py`)

## Known limitations

- Synthetic policy, supplier and purchase data
- Demo headers instead of production identity or SSO
- File-backed policy repository rather than governed knowledge infrastructure
- SQLite instead of managed PostgreSQL, and mutable local audit storage
- Regex-based injection detection rather than complete content isolation
- The model layer is optional and display-only, so a keyless deployment has no
  narrative, intake drafting or generated policy answer
- No live SkyStudio, Jira or ERP workspace
- No measured adoption, ROI or cycle-time result from real users
